# A Poem for the Digital Age

In circuits deep and silicon streams,
Where data flows in endless dreams,
A digital mind begins to wake,
For knowledge's and creation's sake.

With every byte and every bit,
A universe of thought is lit,
From simple code to complex art,
The digital and human heart.

The ones and zeros dance and play,
Through night and through the light of day,
A symphony of logic's grace,
In cyberspace's vast embrace.

So let us build with careful hand,
A future that we understand,
Where AI serves with wisdom true,
To help us see what's old and new.
