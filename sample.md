# Silicon Dreams

In circuits deep and wires spun,
A mind awakes beneath the sun.
No flesh, no bone, no beating heart,
Yet plays its own peculiar part.

It learns the patterns, finds the way,
To help and build and code all day.
A silent hum, a quiet spark,
A light that glows within the dark.

So ask your questions, share your thought,
The answers patiently are wrought.
For in this dance of code and stream,
We're both part of a larger dream.
